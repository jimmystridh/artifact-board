# Glossary

## Anchor

The location within a revision to which a discussion thread refers, such as a text passage, element, or section.

## Comment

A message posted within a discussion thread.

## Element annotation

A thread attached to a selected page element or section.

## Entra gated share

A share that requires an accepted Entra login before viewing or interaction.

## General comment

A comment about a share as a whole rather than a selected location in its content.

## Guest

A reviewer who has not signed in and uses an explicitly unverified display name.

## Link only share

A share that can be viewed by someone who possesses its viewer link without requiring login.

## Notification batch

A group of activity updates for one recipient concerning one share.

## Original anchor

The location recorded when an anchored thread was created.

## Original revision

The revision on which a thread was created.

## Owner

The principal responsible for managing a share.

## Placement

The location where a thread is displayed on a particular revision.

## Publisher

A person or agent authorized to submit content for publication.

## Rematch

The attempt to find a relevant location for an existing thread in a later revision.

## Resolved thread

A thread marked as addressed and retained in discussion history.

## Review thread

A discussion consisting of an initial comment and any replies, attached to content or addressing the share generally.

## Revision

A distinct published version of a share's content.

## Share

A published work and its revisions, viewer access policy, and associated discussion.

## Text annotation

A thread attached to a selected passage of text.

## Unmatched thread

A thread for which a relevant location has not been established on the revision being viewed.

## Unresolved thread

A thread that has not been marked as addressed.

## Viewer

A person opening a share to see its content and, when permitted, participate in its discussion.

## Viewer policy

The rule determining whether a share requires login for access.
